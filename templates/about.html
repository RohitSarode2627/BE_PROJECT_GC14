About Aegis Crime Intelligence

Bridging machine learning, spatial data science, and crime analytics to provide law enforcement and citizens with predictive threat forecasting.

Project Mission

Traditional policing responds to crime after it occurs. Aegis transforms reactive policing into proactive security by identifying high-risk spatial-temporal hotspots before incidents transpire.

Algorithm Pipeline

Our multi-stage pipeline integrates Nominatim OpenStreetMap geocoding, datetime component decomposition, and an ensemble Random Forest model trained on verified urban crime records.

Feature Vector Engineering

Each input query is dynamically encoded into a 7-dimensional normalized feature vector:

  • Month (1-12)
  • Day of Month (1-31)
  • Hour of Day (0-23)
  • Day of Year (1-366)
  • ISO Week of Year (1-53)
  • Latitude (GPS float)
  • Longitude (GPS float)
